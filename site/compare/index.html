Lumina/Compare

Local development tools compared

Lumina, Laragon, XAMPP, Laravel Herd, MAMP and Local all run PHP, a web server and a database on your own computer. They differ in price, in the systems they run on and in what is included. Here they are side by side.

In detail

  • Lumina vs Laragon

    Looking for a Laragon alternative? Lumina is free and open source, runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and gives every project a .test address with trusted HTTPS, like Laragon, plus Node.js and Python dev servers.

  • Lumina vs XAMPP

    Looking for an XAMPP alternative? Lumina is free and open source, with PHP 7.4 to 8.5 side by side, nginx or Apache, MySQL and PostgreSQL, automatic .test sites and trusted HTTPS, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  • Lumina vs Laravel Herd

    Looking for a Laravel Herd alternative? Lumina is free and open source, includes MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, Mailpit and Xdebug at no cost, and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  • Lumina vs MAMP

    Looking for a MAMP or MAMP PRO alternative? Lumina is free and open source, with .test sites, trusted HTTPS, PHP 7.4 to 8.5 per site and MySQL or PostgreSQL, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

  • Lumina vs Local

    Looking for a Local by Flywheel alternative? Lumina is free and open source, runs WordPress and any other PHP, Node.js or Python project, with .test sites and trusted HTTPS.
