Lumina vs Laravel Herd

Laravel Herd is a fast, polished way to run PHP and nginx for Laravel. Its databases, mail catcher and Xdebug are part of Herd Pro, a paid subscription, and there is no Linux version. Lumina includes all of them for free, on all three systems, and is open source.

Why developers switch from Laravel Herd

  • Everything included, for free

    MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, Mailpit, Xdebug and database snapshots, with no subscription.

  • Linux too

    The same app and the same workflow on Windows, macOS and Linux, so a whole team can use it.

  • Not only Laravel

    WordPress, Symfony, Drupal and plain PHP, plus Next.js, Nuxt and Django projects behind their own dev server.

Move your projects from Laravel Herd

  1. Quit Herd (and stop its services), so ports 80 and 443 and the .test domains are free.
  2. Install Lumina and add PHP, nginx and the databases you used from Quick Add.
  3. Move or copy your projects from the Herd folder to Lumina's www folder. They keep their folder.test addresses.
  4. Export your databases (TablePlus, mysqldump or pg_dump) and import them in Lumina.

Questions

Does Lumina work with Laravel?

Yes. QuickApp creates a new Laravel project with its database in one click, and new projects send mail to Mailpit out of the box.

Is there a Pro version of Lumina?

No. Every feature is free and open source under the GNU GPL v3.0.

Does Lumina run on Linux?

Yes: AppImage, .deb and .rpm for x64.
